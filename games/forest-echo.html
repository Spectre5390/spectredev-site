About the Game

Forest Echo is a first-person survival exploration game set in a vast, mystical woodland. Lost and alone, you must use your wits to craft, build, and survive the elements. But you're not entirely alone; uncover the secrets of a lost civilization by interpreting the strange echoes that resonate through the trees.
