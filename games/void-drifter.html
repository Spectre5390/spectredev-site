About the Game

Void Drifter is a single-player space survival roguelike. Pilot your lone vessel through a procedurally generated galaxy, managing scarce resources, upgrading your ship's systems, and evading cosmic horrors. Each jump is a risk, and every discovery could be your last. Can you find your way back to civilization?
