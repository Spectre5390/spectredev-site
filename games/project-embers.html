About the Game

Project Embers is a post-apocalyptic city-builder with a focus on survival and hope. Lead your small band of survivors, scavenge the ruins of civilization, and build a new settlement. Make tough choices, manage resources, and defend your fledgling society from the harsh realities of a world reclaimed by nature.
