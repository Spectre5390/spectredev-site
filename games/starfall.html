About the Game

Starfall is a vibrant open-world action RPG set in a land reshaped by a celestial cataclysm. The fallen "Star Shards" have infused the world with volatile magic. As a shard-bearer, you must harness this power, explore breathtaking landscapes, and confront a dark entity that seeks to corrupt the shards for its own malevolent purpose.
